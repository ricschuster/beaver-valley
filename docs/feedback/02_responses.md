# Feedback responses

Transcribed from the handwritten sheets of the feedback packet
(`01_feedback_packet.md`). One section per reader. Transcribed verbatim except
for light spelling fixes; the reader's clarifications to the transcription are
folded in. The tally at the end counts points raised by three or more readers,
which is the bar for acting on them.

## Reader 1 (Oct 26, 2026)

Cozy-game fan. Has played Stardew Valley for "way more than 250 hrs".

### Sheet 1: first impression

- **Likes in the genre:** farming, collecting, decorating, characters, "and I
  think everything else".
- **What is this game:** "It's like a kind of endless game with no real
  threats, like you die and start over." Meant as passing out and starting a
  new day, as in Stardew Valley. Elsewhere: "like both Stardew Valley
  and Lord of the Rings".
- **Would you play it:** Definitely.
- **Most made you want to play:** "That it's like Stardew Valley and Lord of
  the Rings and it keeps going!"
- **Least made you want to play:** "I don't really understand the hollow crown
  thing. Is he invisible? Or do you not see him?"

### Sheet 2: setting and story

- **Brimmers as a place to spend time:** Yes. Add a festival, a "cake day or
  something", where they dress in food-looking clothes.
- **Hats:** "Very fun, and I love how they love hats (and that you have
  different hats)."
- **Dark lord:** "Sounds right, because it should not be all good guys, maybe
  at least one or two."
- **Curious about the past:** Yes. Wants to know whether the relative is safe
  and what happened to the road.
- **Hall as a restored waystation:** Yes, "it would help the people and make
  them happy".
- **Dawn Carriers as secret wardens:** "fun and cool and mysterious".
- **Ending:** Satisfying. Wanted: "you could be friends with everyone and you
  save everyone from the bad guy".
- **Tone:** "About right."
- **Names:** Brimmers, Thimblemoss, Hearthwell, Dawn Carriers and Hollow Crown:
  Like. The Long Table: Like or Fine (hard to read). Beaver Valley: no verdict.
  The note in the alternative column means "don't call it anything beaver
  related".
- **Reminds you of another book, film or game:** "No, not really."

### Sheet 3: how it plays

- **Day pressure:** "That's the right amount."
- **Farming:** Fun if there are seeds to find and things to grow. It would feel
  like a chore if, when you stop farming for a day (or a bit), people and
  animals get mad at you. In short: no punishment for neglecting farm duties.
- **Specialties and tool upgrades:** Yes, "because you get an upgrade and not
  just never getting better".
- **Bundles ("any 4 of 6"):** "I would enjoy that and rewards would be nice and
  maybe hints?"
- **Rings:** "A good way to unlock stuff and I like it."
- **Expeditions:** New seeds, quests ("go do this to find that"), and stuff to
  decorate your home.
- **Friendship:** Stories, gifts, and maybe new tips or new stuff.
- **Villager count:** "Maybe about 10 to 20 and 40 would be too many."
- **Missing:** Nothing, but a minigame like Junimo Cart in Stardew Valley could
  go in.
- **What would make them stop:** Dying, or something very scary like a creepy
  zombie jumpscare.
- **What would keep them for 40 hours:** "Just what these papers described",
  plus decorating things like their house or character.
- **Anything else:** "There should be lots or a few festivals."

## Notes from reader 1

- Hollow Crown is unclear: unseen or seen? The lore note should say.
- The reader read the game as endless with no real threats. "You die and start
  over" means passing out and starting a new day, so it fits the cozy scope.
- Festivals raised twice (a Brimmer "cake day", and festivals in general).
- Decorating raised three times (genre likes, expeditions, 40-hour question).
  Home and character decoration is outside the first playable's scope.
- Cast size: 10 to 20 is fine, 40 is too many.
- Names: avoid anything beaver related. The lore names all got Like.
- No punishment for neglect: no angry villagers or animals when farm duties are
  skipped.
- No death, no jump scares.
