## Decisions made (all 2026-10-27)

- Cast: ring 1 close to Stardew's size (about 30 named). Rings 2 and 3 add
  people, but only a few with full relationships. Marriage comes later.
- Hollow Crown is never seen directly; his effects are.
- Name: build from the lore names; nothing beaver related.
- Neglect: costs only potential. No villager decay (departs from Stardew).
  Animals follow Stardew: neglect lowers product quality.
- Festivals: two per season, from M3. Hat parade, plus a Brimmer "cake day".
- Bundle hints: M3 candidate.
- Decoration: Stardew-like. Rewards from bundles, tailoring, hats first.
- Minigames: a few, optional and cozy, a festival game first.
- Tone: no scares, no lose state.
- Marriage: Stardew-shaped, with player-only divorce, no spouse anger.
