## Completed

- **Touch controls (#226), `27_touch_controls.md`:** tap-to-move paths around
  rocks, trees, water and fences, and a blocked target turns red and the farmer
  stays put. The toolbar takes taps only on a slot, with a dead margin round it,
  so an edge tap walks the farmer and never changes the tool. The toolbar sits
  along the bottom in both schemes, and Eat appears beside it when food is
  selected (it takes the action button's place in the stick scheme). Only the
  real-tablet check is left (#59, #63).
- **Bundle rewards pass, part one (#227), issue #201 stays open:** bundle 27
  gives a crystalarium (the owner's choice, replacing the dropped duplicator),
  bundle 19 gives 20 coal since bundle 5 already gives the charcoal kiln, and
  bundle 12 keeps its keg. The machine set is now twelve (`24`). `22`, `07` and
  the design map follow.
- **`33_build_order.md` (#228):** five slices, one hall room each. Slice 1 is
  year one on the farm, all four seasons, as in Stardew (the owner chose this
  over a two season first slice). Slice 2 fishing and the Water room. Slice 3
  the mines proper. Slice 4 animals, cooking and friendship. Slice 5 the Last
  Seat. Ring 2 stays M4. `03`, `13` and `26` point at it.
- **`34_crafting.md` (#228):** crafting follows Stardew, a pause menu tab plus
  a workbench bought from the carpenter (2,000g) that uses adjacent chests.
  Fences do not decay. The list covers storage, fences, paths, light,
  sprinklers, fertilizer and every machine cost, each with a source and a slice.
- **Battery pack (#229):** a thunderstorm weather type is added and a lightning
  rod turns a strike into one battery pack. Lightning never harms the farm.
  This reverses the line in `31_farm_growth.md` that lightning does not exist.
  Lamp-posts (bundle 20) are fillable once storms exist.
- **Bars in slice 1 (#230):** the first part of the mines moves into slice 1, as
  in Stardew: floors 1 to 10 with no creatures, the lift, copper, the opening on
  spring day 5, the smith dwarf's ore shop and the furnace gift. Iron and gold
  ore come from her shop until slice 3. Bundles 19 and 20 become fillable in
  slice 1. Slice 3 finishes no room.
