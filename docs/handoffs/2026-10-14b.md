## Next actions

1. **Start here: the bigger game questions.** Go through them one at a time
   with the person leading the project, starting with 1 and 2. They are what
   `00_project_brief.md` asks for, and everything else depends on them.

   1. **Identity.** What makes this game its own? Why would someone who owns
      Stardew Valley play this? What feeling should a good evening with it
      leave? Is the twist mainly setting, a mechanic (expeditions) or tone?
   2. **Audience and size.** Personal project, free hobby release or
      commercial Steam game? This decides how much the EULA, license and name
      matter. How many people, how many hours a week? How long is a full
      playthrough? (The current pacing target is about 4 in-game years.)
   3. **The player's story.** Why is the player character here? Is there an
      ending or a "you have made it" moment? Does the ambient threat have a
      story or is it atmosphere?
   4. **The core loop of one day.** What does a day look like, and what is the
      main trade-off the player makes each day?
   5. **Emphasis.** Which gets the most weight: farming and crafting depth,
      relationships and story, or expeditions (and possibly combat)?
   6. **Single player or multiplayer.** Multiplayer has to be planned from the
      start or is very costly to add later.

2. Fill in `00_project_brief.md` from the answers.
3. Revisit the hook, the spatial structure and the community project chain
   against the brief. Then return to centre 3 (issue #5) and bundle formats.
4. Pick a small first playable and record it in `docs/design/03_milestones.md`.
5. Choose the engine and language and record it in an ADR.
