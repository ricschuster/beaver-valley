  saves carry the trees. The playtest scripts follow the new slot order.
- **Revision (#307), after the owner reviewed my choices:**
  - A full bag drops goods on the ground within 3 tiles, saved. Walking within

    rings and buffs). This was the owner's idea, and matches Stardew's
    magnetism.
  - The hoe and can lift seedlings, and the hoe lifts a planted seed. The hand
    does not.
  - Holding Use carries on from the felled tree to its stump.
  - Swing counts are Stardew's Axe page: 10 a tree and 5 a stump at tier 0, then
    8/4, 6/3, 4/2, 2/1.
- Read the saved wiki pages (Axe, Trees, Mobile Controls, Rings). Stumps are
  confirmed. Stardew's full-pack behaviour is not on them.
