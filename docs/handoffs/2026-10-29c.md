# Session Handoff: 2026-10-29 (twenty-ninth session)

## Summary

A design session. No code changed. The session finished the fishing open list
with the owner: rods, bait, lures, who sells them, how a fish is gated, the
Magpie Hook treasure, the shellfish pots and the legend seasons. Fishing design
is now as complete as it can be before a playtest and a real tablet.

## Completed

- Four rods, three baits and six lures (`21_fishing.md`, "Rods, bait and
  lures"). Rods: Reed Pole 50g, Hazel Rod 400g, Heron Rod 1500g, Hearthwell Rod
  4000g. They differ in bar, range and slots (none, bait, bait and one lure,
  bait and two lures). Lures last 20 casts: Glint, Moth, Storm, Whirligig, Wide
  Float and Magpie Hook.
- Gating, after Stardew (where rods mostly do not gate species): only the Reed
  Pole is limited, to common fish. Rare fish need fishing level 5. Legends need
  the Hearthwell Rod. The first draft gated every rod by rarity; the owner chose
  the starter-only gate.
- The delta fisher sells rods, bait and lures. `20_ring1_cast.md` now lists her
  as the fisher (she may also keep the ferry). The Beach has only the odd elf.
- Magpie Hook treasure: a coin purse (`40 + 15 * level` gold) 6 times in 10,
  else a trinket worth 30 to 120g. No double catch.
- Shellfish pots: eight catches, four fresh and four salt, one catch a day, no
  bait, no season gate. Bundle 18 takes 5 of the 8.
- Legend seasons spread: spring Grandmother Hush, summer Great Hearthfin, autumn
  the Valley King and the Hearthwell Carp, winter Moonlit Mother.
- Bundle 17 rare picks confirmed: Silverthread, Hermit Trout, Crowned Tunny.
- A new Fishing section in `07_tuning_numbers.md`: rod, bait, lure, pot, fish
  and legend prices, XP, the quality roll and the Pearl Lobster odds.
