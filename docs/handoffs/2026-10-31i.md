# Session Handoff: 2026-10-31 (forty-third session)

## Summary

A design session. No code changed. It settled the four open items from the
last handoff: the rare boost items, the slayer list, ring 2's own completion
total, and ring 1's finishing scene and keepsake. Each question had a Stardew
option. The notes are in PRs #255, #256, #257 and #258, all merged, plus this
handoff.

## Completed

- **Elderfruit (#255, #256, `39_completion_total.md`):** the rare boost item is
  called elderfruit (plain English, a gift of the old elves). Seven, +34 maximum
  energy each, from seven ring 1 sources: the Harvest Showing prize counter (fall
  9, placeholder 2,000g), a chest in floor 120's last hall, a spouse at 12.5
  hearts (one only), the hermit's high-price sale (placeholder 20,000g), a Secret
  Woods spirit for the fruit of a rare seed the hermit gives, the fish mail, and
  the full gallery set. "Find every stardrop" in `38_achievements.md` counts
  them, and its hat is the Elderfruit Circlet.
- **Slayer list (#255, #256, `22_mines.md`):** twelve goals, one per creature
  kind, Stardew's counts by matching role (Gloop 1,000 down to Pebbleback 30),
  each giving a ring or a worn hat (not an achievement hat), rebuyable from the
  warden head. Lanternmoth gives a warrior ring because a bundle already gives
  the glow ring. `37_equipment.md` and `14_festival_calendar.md` are updated.
- **Ring 2's total (#257):** a 130-item collectible at weight 25 (100 open a
  room), plus centre 2 bundles 20, new fish and finds 10, deep dig slayer list
  10, elf friends 10, new items shipped 10, recipes 5, crafting 5, special orders
  board 5. Reward is a haven hat, scene and keepsake. No ring 2 design note
  exists yet (#20), so the weights are placeholders.
- **Ring 1's finish (#258):** a short hall scene (about two minutes). The hall
  steward gives a toast, Old Greencoat adds a line towards ring 2, and each
  household head says one line. Villagers wear the hats the player gave them.
  The keepsake is a lamp that never gutters, furniture by mail.
- The design map (`docs/overview.json`) has four new entries, all under #196.
