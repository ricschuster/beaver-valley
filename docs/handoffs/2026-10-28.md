# Session Handoff: 2026-10-28 (twenty-first session)

## Summary

A design session on the village hall. The owner answered the open questions
from the twentieth handoff, and the full hall was designed and written down in
`docs/design/16_bundles.md`: six rooms, 30 bundles, rewards, a count-based soft
gate and one gate to ring 2. No game code changed. Every item, count and reward
is a placeholder.

## Completed

- #166: `docs/design/16_bundles.md` (proposed), plus placeholder numbers in
  `07_tuning_numbers.md` and two fixes in `09_story_and_lore.md`. Merged. It
  refs #21, which is still open.
- Decisions made with the owner (2026-10-28):
  - One gate to ring 2. The last room mends the bridge and opens the road, with
    no second room behind it.
  - The last room is called **The Last Seat**. The hall keeps the name The
    Long Table.
  - Rooms appear by the total number of bundles finished: Foragers' Shelf at
    the start, Larder and Water room after 1, Hearth after 2, Guests' Board
    after 3, The Last Seat after 20 of the other 26.
  - Bundle counts follow Stardew: 6, 6, 6, 3, 5 and 4 (30 in total).
  - Room unlocks: Foragers' Shelf gives Old Greencoat's herb and seed stall,
    Larder the greenhouse, Water room the ferry and a lake cove, Hearth carts
    between farm, hall, town and mines, Guests' Board the hat standing boost,
    and The Last Seat the bridge.
  - Each bundle gives a useful item. Rewards lean towards machines and tools,
    seeds and consumables, and wearable equipment (glow ring, magnet ring).
  - The Last Seat asks for finished goods from three or four rooms. Its last
    bundle, The Long Road, gives a letter to the elves from Old Greencoat.
  - Ring 1 has a coast, so the Sea Fish bundle stays. Traps work in fresh and
    salt water.
  - The mines open early on a fixed day. A rockfall blocks them until Old
    Greencoat comes to the door. The Hearth's carts link them, but the Hearth
    does not unlock them.
- Lore note fixes: ring 2 now opens with the last room (not "after bundle 3 of
  5"), and ring 1 has no whispers of the Smoulderer.
