A design session. No code changed. It worked through items 1 to 3 of the
previous handoff (the farm types note, the processing and cooking note, and the

owner settled each open question as a decision. It also added a standing rule

## Completed

- **Standing rule (#222):** every question to the owner must include an option
  that is what Stardew does, labelled "(Stardew)". If the Stardew behaviour is
  not known or not checked, the option says so. Written in `CLAUDE.md` under
  working rules.
- **`32_farm_types.md` (#219):** six types in the long run, Standard and
  Woodland first. A farm is a map plus a record of rule changes. Meadow starts
  with a built coop and two chickens (an exception to `23_animals.md`, which now
  points to it). Fish tables: a farm names a split of existing tables, Riverside
  uses river and woods, every other farm has a small pond on the river table.
  The choice screen is on the new game screen and locked for the save.
- **`24_processing_and_cooking.md` (#221, replaces #220):** a hall bundle gives
  the first machine, then more are craftable. Eleven machines: the original
  eight plus a seed maker, a dye vat and a fish smoker. The duplicator is
  dropped. The mayonnaise machine and oil maker come from farming level
  recipes (level 2 and 8). Craft costs use Stardew's lists. The kitchen comes
  with the first farmhouse upgrade (10,000g and 450 wood). The inn kitchen sells
  a few recipes and dishes like Stardew's saloon. A level 10 profession choice
  for every skill. The cask is later. The recipe target is 81, the saved
  Cooking page's count, spread over rings 1 to 3 (about 40, 25 and 16).
- **`21_fishing.md` (#223, #224):** Stardew's five bar behaviours with a
  difficulty on every fish. A first catch that cannot fail. Fish lengths, "New
  record!" and "First Catch!". Water plants and jellies with no bar. Trash with
  a recycler at fishing level 4. The hooked fish is hidden, except a crown on
  legends, with a new Sounding Float lure (seven lures now). Stardew's fishing
  professions. Fish ponds later, in their own note. Pots now work as in
  Stardew: one bait a day and 38 in 100 junk (30 with Fine Bait), which reverses
  the first draft's bait-free pot and makes Mariner and Luremaster work.
- Design map (`docs/overview.json`, `docs/overview.html`), the system review
  (`25`), `30_farm_types.md` and `07_tuning_numbers.md` updated to match.

- The seed maker and fish smoker recipe sources in Stardew are not checked (the
  saved pages do not cover them).

  feel like a tax, and that the Pearl Lobster (about 12 in 100 a day per pot)
  is not too easy with three pots.
- Trash from rods comes with no bar, which differs from Stardew. Check that
  trash does not feel like a wasted bite.
- Hearthsilver as the top grade name is still unconfirmed (see 2026-10-31a).
- Weeds that never destroy items is softer than Stardew. Check pressure to weed
  in a playtest.
- Fiber and Sap must exist as items before sprinkler and fertilizer recipes.
- Four seasons widen M3. Bundles need a rule so a missed season never locks the
  player out (`26_seasons.md`).
- Wiki and Guidebook text is reference only and must not be copied into the repo
