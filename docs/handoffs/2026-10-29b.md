  added, and Crowned Tunny now runs Summer and Autumn.
- Five legends, one per water, worth a lot as the fish itself, each catchable
  again each year: the Hearthwell Carp (cove, autumn, dusk in rain, the only
  cove legend), Grandmother Hush (forest lake, spring), the Valley King (river,
  summer), Moonlit Mother (mountain lake, winter, night, clear) and Great
  Hearthfin (sea, summer, day, any weather). The Carp opens when the Water room
  is done. The other four open after bundles 13 to 18 are done.
- The Pearl Lobster fills the shellfish slot of bundle 17. Sea, any season,
  rare on the rod, more common in a salt water pot, never required.
- A game-wide completion tracker (Perfection-style) is recorded as wanted. All
  fish and legends count toward it. Issue #196, item 13b of the note.
- PR #197 carries all of this, with auto-merge on.
