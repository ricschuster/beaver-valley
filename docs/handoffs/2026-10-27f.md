## Bundle design agreed in conversation (not yet written)

- Full centre 1 is five or six rooms with three to five bundles each. The
  current five bundles become the first slice.
- Rooms so far: the Larder (crops, preserves, animals later, unlocks the
  greenhouse), the Foragers' Shelf (seasonal forage, plus wood, stone and
  fibre), the Water room (fishing), the Hearth (metal, lamps, cloth, crafts;
  tool tiers and minecarts), the Guests' Board (cooking, dyes, hospitality; a
  hat standing boost and festival extras) and the Long Table (the last room).
- The bridge must be the hardest and latest thing in ring 1. The suggestion is
  that finishing the Long Table mends the bridge and opens the road to ring 2,
  as one gate with no second room behind it. The owner asked what this meant
  and has not decided it.
- The Foragers' Shelf lost the bridge, so the suggested unlock is Old
  Greencoat setting up a herb and seed stall at the hall.
- The Water room replaces Stardew's panning (the owner dislikes it). The
  suggestions are a repaired ferry to a lake cove inside ring 1, or fish ponds.
  Not chosen yet.
- Minecarts need mines inside ring 1. The owner wants combat in the mines. The
  tone must stay gentle (cartoonish creatures, a soft penalty when beaten).
  Mines and fishing each need their own design note before anyone builds them.
- A soft gate, as in Stardew's Boiler Room, is suggested: the Long Table opens
  only after a number of other rooms are done.
