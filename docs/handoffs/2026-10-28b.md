## Completed

PRs #168 to #174, all merged, all on `09_story_and_lore.md`.

- Hermit (#168): she is now a witch (she/her), no longer a wizard. The pencil
  witch in `15_character_art.md` is her starting look.
- Old Greencoat and the Wanderer are one character (#168). The notes say "the
  Wanderer", valley folk say "Old Greencoat". The hermit is a different person.
- Warden head (#168): a Qi-type figure, the leader of the couriers (the Dawn
  Carriers), a third person apart from the Wanderer. The owner likened her to
  Aragorn, as shorthand for feel only (ADR 0003). The rule that the wardens
  must not read as Tolkien's Rangers still holds. Written as a woman for now.
- Warden head reveal (#168, proposed): she appears early and is revealed in
  stages, as Qi is. Ring 1 first year: a watchful courier at the carrier post,
  no name. Ring 1 later: mystery notes signed with a mark, and an alias. Start
  of ring 2: the Dawn Carriers reveal themselves and she is their head. Ring 2
  onward: her past. Ring 3: the long arc closes. Everything is optional and
  costs nothing to skip. The Dawn Carriers as a group stay unmentioned in
  ring 1.
- Cast balance (#168, #169): a table of set characters and every open NPC slot
  with a suggested gender (a proposal). About seven women and five men, plus
  mixed groups.
- Fairy rings (#170, #171): the hermit's warps are grown rings of toadstools on
  the farm, not obelisks. Four: mountain (the dwarves' mountains), beach,
  island (the elf part of ring 2) and one for ring 3. A fifth is held open for
  a second ring 2 region (#20). The Hearth's carts already cover farm, hall,
  town and mines.
- Getting back (#170 to #172): rings only go out from the farm. Consumable warp
  totems take the player home. The permanent version is the Homeward pin, an
  inventory item that takes a slot and is used from the toolbar. It is not a
  hat, because hats are a feature (hat standing). The name is ours, not the
  Stardew one.
- Pin seller (#173, #174): an odd one-off elf, a woman, not an elf house head.
  She lives by the sea in ring 1, so she is the first elf the player meets. She
  sells the pin at a friendship level, in the way of Krobus's shop. Her
  backstory so far: she was leaving by ship and found she could not leave yet.
  The harbour is shorthand for feel only.
