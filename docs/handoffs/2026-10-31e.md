## Completed

- **Clay and quartz (#232), `34_crafting.md`:** clay comes from hoeing dirt
  patches on mine floors 1 to 10 (placeholder 5 percent) and rarely from geodes.
  Quartz lies on the floor of those floors and rarely comes from geodes. Farm
  soil gives no clay. Both follow Stardew's mines.
- **Bundle pools (#233), new `35_bundle_pools.md`:** pools for bundles 1 to 10,
  12 and 19, with draft fall crops, fall forage, winter forage and rare finds.
  Decisions: the greenhouse arrives in slice 4 with the animal bundle, as in
  Stardew (the Larder needs bundle 11); bundle 20 leaves slice 1 (oil needs the
  oil maker); bundle 12's pool avoids keg goods because its reward is the keg.
  `16`, `33` and the design map follow.
- **Fall and winter numbers (#234), `07_tuning_numbers.md`:** fall crops match
  the saved Stardew Fall Crops page (pumpkin, eggplant, sweet potato, beet,
  cranberry, artichoke, plus corn in fall). Prices and energy for fall, winter
  and rare forage. Winter soil digging gives frost root and snow tuber, as in
  Stardew (placeholder 3 percent a tile).
- **Storm and weather numbers (#235), `07_tuning_numbers.md`:** fall rain 20
  percent, winter snow 30 percent and no winter storms. A quarter of rain days
  are storms. Summer 13 and 26 are always stormy. Strikes fall through the day (3
  percent per 10 minutes while awake) and use Stardew's catch formula. A hit rod
  makes one pack the next morning. A rod has no reach, which corrects the
  eight tile guess in `34_crafting.md`.
- **Lightning rod (#236):** it keeps Stardew's bat wing from the Cave Bat, so
  rods, battery packs and lamp-posts move to slice 3. Slice 1 storms are weather
  only. Moss is a renewable tree good (saved Trees page), so it left bundle 6,
  where wild ginger replaced it.
