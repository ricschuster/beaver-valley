## Current state

- `npm run check` passes; 67 tests across `tests/`.
- Everything under `src/core/` is plain TypeScript. Content lives in
  `src/content/*.json` (crops, forage, bundles). Item ids: produce uses the
  crop or forage id, seeds are `<crop>-seed`.
- `GameState` (`src/core/save.ts`) holds day, money, inventory, farm, skills,
  hall, weather and the unsaved forage field. The scene should hold one,
  call the action functions, call `sleep(g, spots, random)` at day end, then
  `saveGame`.
- `FarmScene` does not use `GameState` yet, and has no map, player, tools,
  hall, shop or end screen.
- Known simplifications: no tool upgrades or skill perks, no seasonal forage
  beyond the two lists, no forecast, forage is not saved, saves are between
  days only. Bundle items and rewards are placeholders.
