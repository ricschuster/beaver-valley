# Session Handoff: 2026-10-14 (fourth session)

## Summary

Organisation and first design decisions session. We set up a roadmap and
tracker so the overall picture and the next payoffs are easy to find between
sessions, then settled the core loop of a day (issue #14) and the ring 2 and 3
leanings. No code or stack choice yet. All design decisions are draft.

## Completed

- Roadmap: `docs/design/03_milestones.md` (M1 to M5, payoffs, blockers, and a
  proposed first playable scope) and a filled-in `CLAUDE.md` status section.
- GitHub setup: labels `design-question` and `next-payoff`, milestones
  `M1-define-first-playable` to `M5-ring-3-centre-3`, and issues #12 to #21
  for the open questions. Issue #5 is labelled and on M5.
- Ring leanings recorded in the system map: ring 2 is a haven plus about 2
  regions; the ring 3 bastion is logistics only (no defence, no time pressure);
  travel leans to Option A (first story journey, then waystation day trips).
  Option B (outposts to sleep away) is not ruled out. Noted on issue #5.
- Issue #14, core loop of a day, settled and closed:
  - Energy plus a clock. Running out means passing out, with a small cost.
  - The clock stops in menus and conversations (single player). Day length and
    speed are our own tunable numbers, not copied from the original.
  - Skill categories: farming, foraging, fishing, mining, leveled by doing.
    Combat stays out for now.
  - First playable: foraging plus farming with skills, and two short seasons
    (eventually four full seasons).
  - A typical day: morning chores, a main stretch choosing between foraging and
    farming, an evening of selling and hand-ins. No shop hours or schedules.
- PRs merged: #10, #11, #22 to #27 (handoff, ring notes, roadmap, status, day
  cycle, activities and skills, seasons, typical day).

  its scope before building.
- The Steam EULA was never read.

  `docs/decisions/README.md` says `NNNN-short-title.md`. We followed the README.

  it. Issue #13 affects this.
- A real name is needed eventually. A name ending in "Valley" sits close to the
  original's branding.
- Handoff filenames use the UTC date. This file is `2026-10-14d.md` because
  handoffs for that date already exist.
