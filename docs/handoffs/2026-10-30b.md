## Processing and cooking: recommendations awaiting the owner

Artisan Goods, Cooking, Crafting and Fertilizer were read this session. Nothing
is decided yet. Recommendations given:

1. Machines come from hall bundles, as already planned, not from skill levels.
2. Eight machines, one input and one batch each: preserves jar, keg, cheese
   press, mayonnaise machine, loom, oil maker, bee house, charcoal kiln. The
   furnace and kiln are already designed in `22_mines.md`.
3. Leave out the cask, dehydrator, fish smoker and seed maker for now. The cask
   is the first to bring back, for aged wine and cheese.
4. Most goods ignore ingredient quality and sell for about 2 to 3 times the raw
   item. Wine is the best fruit use, jam suits cheap fruit.
5. Cloth is the one exception: better wool gives a better chance of 2 cloth.
6. Kitchen unlocks with the first farmhouse upgrade, with a portable cookout kit
   at foraging level 3. Recipes come from friendship mail, the inn kitchen and a
   few cookbooks. One timed food buff, plus a separate drink slot.
7. About 30 to 35 dishes for the first version, our own names and flavour, with
   buffs only for stats we have.
8. Artisan profession: 40 percent more on machine goods except oil and coffee.
   Needs the level 10 choice screen.
9. Keep fertilizer out of this note. It is a farming feature.
