## Fish page findings (not yet in a note)

Where we match Stardew: a bar minigame, quality lift on a perfect catch, bites
set by water, season, time and weather, five legends, shellfish traps that are
never required, about 46 fish.

Things Stardew has and `21_fishing.md` does not:

- Named bar behaviours (mixed, smooth, sinker, floater, dart) and a difficulty
  per fish. Our note says each fish needs a bar motion but defines none.
- A first catch that cannot fail.
- Fish lengths, a "New record!" and a "First Catch!" message.
- Non-fish catches picked up with no minigame (seaweed, algae, jellies).
- Trash and a recycler.
- A way to see which fish is hooked.
- Fish ponds, and a fish price profession.

Gaps in our own note: no sell prices for the ordinary fish in `21_fishing.md`
(not checked in `07_tuning_numbers.md`), whether pots give fishing XP, and
whether the Magpie Hook treasure affects the perfect catch.
