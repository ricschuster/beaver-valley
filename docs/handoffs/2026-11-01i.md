## Results

| Run | Wall time | "could not reach" | Walk retries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| wood.mjs 1x | 235 s | 0 | |
| wood.mjs 4x | 66 s | 0 | |
| expert 4x, 84 days | 9,774 s (163 min) | 67 | 270 |
| expert 1x, to day 16 | 1,035 s (17 min) | 3 | 14 |

- The rules are the same at both speeds (wood run: 3 days to 450 wood, about
  27 energy a tree).
- Cause (a reading, not tested): the game moves whole frames, so a step is
  about 1.3 x N px. The bot polls the position from outside and cannot stop
  within a tile reliably. The same tiles fail again and again (18,12, 16,23,
  6,21, 31,25) and each failure costs about 85 s of retries.
- 8x fails at once (cannot stop on the narrow house door tile 6,6).
- A fresh-game probe walks to 31,25 fine at 1x and 4x, so the failures depend
  on the state later in a run (crops, trees).
- The 1x bot also fails about 1 day in 5 on a walk (days 8, 9 and 16 here).
  That is an old bot weakness, not from this work.
