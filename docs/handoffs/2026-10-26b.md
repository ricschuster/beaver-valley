- PR #118 (check it merged): the draft lore note `09_story_and_lore.md`.
- Decisions from the talk, all recorded in the lore note:
  - Emphasis: farming and crafting, then expeditions, then relationships.
    Relationships matter from the first week, as in Stardew Valley, so a saved
    friendship value, gifts and early events belong in M3. Ring 2 adds people but
    does not gate the system. The long-term cast is about Stardew's size, built
    in layers as data.
  - Story: the player inherits a farm from a vanished, quiet and shrewd
    gardener-adventurer. A kingdom fell to a dark lord, who was beaten and
    banished far away. The valley survived by being overlooked. The hall is the
    old waystation (inn and granary). Secret wardens pass as couriers.
  - Ending: no real ending, a celebration when the last centre 1 bundle is done.
  - Rings: ring 2 opens partway through the chain, when the bridge bundle mends
    the old bridge, and each region then needs its own tool or bridge. Ring 3
    stays behind centre 2. Ring 1 is peaceful and the shadow first shows up
    when the road opens.
  - Names: the folk are Brimmers (small, long-eared, elaborate hats, favour
    ledgers, very hospitable). The family name is Thimblemoss. The wardens are
    the Dawn Carriers. The crossroads town is Hearthwell and the hall is The
    Long Table. The dark lord is a Hollow Crown with shadow and a great voice
    (working title). The first festival is a hat parade.
  - The relative leaves a trunk of seeds and one plain welcome note. A villager
    nudges the player toward the hall, as the mayor does in Stardew.
