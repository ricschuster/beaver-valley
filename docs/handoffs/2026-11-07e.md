## Completed

- **#500, sim bot.** Plays the 112 day year. It farms through fall and winter,
  clears weeds to find soil, chops trees, breaks rocks on the farm and mine floor
  1, buys ore and coal, runs the furnace and keeps gold back for seeds. Only
  Artisan Goods is skipped (jar and bee house runs, an orchard). Expert medians:
  Smith's day 57, Woodland day 73, Autumn Crops 65, all played bundles day 88,
  9 of 10 runs finish.
- **#501, quartz.** A one swing crystal node on mine floors 1 to 10 (6 percent of
  rocks), item sells 25g, unlocks the quality sprinkler recipe.
- **#502, pickaxe tiers.** Hoe, can and pickaxe upgrade at the smith for gold plus
  5 bars, with a 2 day wait, a morning notice and the tool refused while away.
  The shop's tool rows moved to the smith screen. Pickaxe swings per rock fall
  with the grade.
- **#503, crafted furnaces.** A placeable machine, mining level 4, 20 copper ore
  and 25 stone.
- **#504, chests and the workbench.** Four chests (36 and 70 slots) on the farm or
  in the house, a chest screen, a 2,000g workbench from the carpenter whose
  crafting draws on the chests beside it.
- **#505, Old Greencoat and the camp chest.** News at the door on spring day 5, and
  a take only gift chest on floor 10.
- **#506, geodes.** Nodes, the smith cracks them for 25g into stone, ore, quartz,
  placeholder gems or a found thing. Rockhound adds geodes.
- **#507, achievements.** Thirteen measurable goals, each unlocking a hat sold at
  the hatter's counter (on a new screen reached from Hats).
