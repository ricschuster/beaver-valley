## Completed

- `28_shipping_bin.md` (#213): the bin and the shop both pay the same price. The
  bin pays at sleep, including after a pass-out, with the price fixed when the
  item is shipped. Anything shipped can be taken back until sleep. One bin comes
  with every farm. A day-end summary lists what sold. No sell warning.
- `29_weeds.md` (#214): weeds land on day 1 of spring, summer and fall, and
  winter clears them. They spread to free tiles but never destroy anything. Any
  tool clears one. A cut weed gives 50 percent Fiber, else 5 percent a mixed
  seed. Farm only for now. `26_seasons.md` updated.
- `30_farm_types.md` (#215): reference check of the farm type decisions against
  the Farm Maps page and Guidebook printed pages 12 and 13. No conflicts. The
  Guidebook spread is on PDF page 5, not 4.
- `31_farm_growth.md` (#216, #217): three sprinkler tiers with the nozzle and
  enricher add-ons, no crows (decorative scarecrows later), fruit trees as in
  Stardew in M3, two fertilizer tiers, the greenhouse as the Larder room reward
  with a Stardew-style layout, and the farm cave as a permanent bat or mushroom
  choice at a total earnings mark. Fertilizer and add-ons are toolbar items.
  Sap comes from weeds, grass and the woods.
- The system review (`25`), seasons note (`26`) and design map
  (`docs/overview.json`, `docs/overview.html`) point to the new notes.
