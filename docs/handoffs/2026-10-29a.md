## Completed

  candidates, any player can court any, no pairing limits, one spouse at a
  time. Flowers court, a ring-like token that is not worn proposes. Spouse
  helps are small, divorce is player-only with a small fee and friendship stays,
  children are by player choice behind a house upgrade, no roommate, and the
  spouse stays on the farm.
- Risks (PR #192). Humans stay visitors in Hearthwell. A marsh lake is added in
  the River delta, so three lakes are open from the start, and no Water room
  bundle may need cove fish. The warden head's reveal is now six stages with no
  mailbox: a mid-ring glimpse at the carrier post, then a farm visit the morning
  after the bridge is mended (#177). The Homeward pin opens at 3 hearts at a high
  price (#176). Comments were added on #176, #177 and #181.
- CI fix (PR #193). `tests/tools.test.ts` rolled real crop quality and failed
  about one run in thirty on `main`. It now pins `Math.random`.
- Fishing (`21_fishing.md`, this PR). A softened Stardew-style bar with two
  assist levels, a cast distance, a third skill, Stardew-style quality from a
  size roll plus a perfect-catch bonus, its own rod line, bait spent per cast,
  lures, shellfish-only optional pots, about 45 fish, and a legend fish caught
  in the cove at dusk in rain. Bundle 17 swaps the legend for a rare shellfish.
  The Catch (28) stays, reachable on assist through the level roll.
- Issue #194 opened for a fifth tool tier above gold, from a deep mine metal.
