## Current state

- The house-door failure was the script, not the game. On the device the
  position poll lags a few frames, so full-speed steps overshot the one-tile
  doorway by 3 to 4 px and the player stopped against the wall.
- Device runs are fragile in two ways. Stale game tabs on the tablet make the
  script drive a background tab (the stick moves 0 px). A run killed mid-gesture
  can leave a finger down for the next run. Close extra tabs and rerun.
- Not checked: how the controls feel under a finger, and the keyboard personas

  cannot answer the profession scene.
- The keyboard hint line ("Move: arrows/WASD...") shows on the tablet and
  overlaps the house roof. It is a desktop hint on a touch device.
