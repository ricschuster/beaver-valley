# Session Handoff: 2026-10-31 (fortieth session)

## Summary

A design session. No code changed. It settled fiber, sap and common trees,
filled the weed and tree growth numbers, and designed the equipment slots. It
ended with a list of what slice 1 still needs. Each open question was settled
by the owner as a decision, with a Stardew option offered each time, and the
owner chose the Stardew option every time. Notes are in PRs #238 to #241, all
merged.

## Completed

- **Fiber, sap and common trees (#238), new `36_gathering_trees_sap_fiber.md`:**
  fiber comes from weeds only (50 percent), and grass gives hay only. Sap comes
  from chopping common trees (5) and removing stumps (1), not from weeds, grass
  or forage. This replaces the sap source in `31_farm_growth.md`. Common trees
  follow the saved Trees page, with one kind in slice 1 and no tappers.
- **Tree growth (#239):** Stardew's, from the saved Trees page. A seedling has
  20 percent a night to reach the next stage, stage 4 takes twice as long, the
  median is 24 days, there is no growth in winter, and tree fertilizer gives 5
  days. My first draft guessed 7 days a stage, since I had not read that far.
- **Weeds (#240):** a fixed 20 weeds on day 1 of spring, summer and fall, capped
  by free tillable tiles, with spread 10 percent a night (20 in rain, 15 in
  summer). These are ours. No Stardew count or spread chance exists in the
  saved Weeds, Grass, Gold Clock and Modding Maps pages or in an online search.
  The placeholder farm has only 96 tillable tiles, so a per-tile rate gave 2
  weeds there.
- **Equipment (#241), new `37_equipment.md`:** two ring slots, boots, hat,
  shirt and pants. Boots give Defense and Immunity. The ring list is Stardew's,
  minus the wedding and jukebox rings. Rings can be combined at the forge. A
  tap-to-equip "Wear" tab is ours. Slice 3 builds the slot, with the glow and
  magnet bundle rings first.
- Linked notes, `07_tuning_numbers.md` and the design map were updated.
