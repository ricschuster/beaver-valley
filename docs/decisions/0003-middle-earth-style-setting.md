# Middle-earth style setting, original content

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The setting should feel like the world of The Lord of the Rings: an
old-fashioned high fantasy land of shires, forests, ruins and distant peril.
The works and their names are protected by the Tolkien Estate and its
licensees, and we found no fan-content policy that allows a game built on
them. The release is a free hobby game (issue #13), which does not remove that
risk.

## Decision

We take the feel and the genre conventions, not the content. The setting is
Middle-earth style, with its own world, place names, peoples, characters, text
and lore. General fantasy tropes (hobbit-like homely villagers, old roads,
ruins, a distant shadow) are fine when written our own way. We do not use
names, characters, places, quotations, maps, languages or artwork from the
books, the films or other adaptations, and we do not call the game or world
Middle-earth or use any Tolkien name.

## Consequences

- The world, names and lore are invented and recorded in `docs/design/`.
- Reviewing a name or idea for closeness to Tolkien's content is part of
  design, in the same way as for Stardew Valley (ADR 0002).
- Using anything directly from the source material would need a new ADR that
  supersedes this one, and a check of the rights involved first.
