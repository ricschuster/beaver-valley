# 20 Ring 1 cast

Status: **structure decided with the owner on 2026-10-28.** The roles, races,
genders, households and which people are marriage candidates are set in the
tables below. Names, ages, looks and voices are not set. It answers the
structure half of issue #136 and feeds #137 (marriage candidates). Nothing here
is built.

## Why

Ring 1 aims at about 30 named villagers (`09_story_and_lore.md`), and only
about a dozen had a role. The valley layout (`19_valley_layout.md`) gave every
person a place. This note fills the places with people, so the dialogue
(`18_dialogue.md`) and voice sheet work has a cast to write.

The cast was worked out in `docs/tools/cast-planner.html`, a single page that
opens from disk. It keeps edits in the browser and produces a text summary to
copy out. Reset it to its defaults to see this note's cast.

## Decisions

1. **30 named people**: 8 outside Hearthwell, the courier, and 21 in 11
   Hearthwell households.
2. **Shops are staffed by the households that live above or behind them.**
   Other households can work in a shop part-time, as in Stardew's saloon and
   clinic. Each shop family is one interior map (shop and home together).
3. **The courier lodges at the inn.** The carrier post courier is the warden
   head in her early form. The lore stages are unchanged: she is shown to be
   the head of the Dawn Carriers when ring 2 opens.
4. **Races are human, elf, dwarf, wizard and halfling.** The Brimmers are
   halflings (`09_story_and_lore.md`, "Type"). Road folk are humans. The
   Wanderer and the hermit are wizards.
5. **Children: four in total**, each in a household. They stay on the standard
   dialogue tier.
6. **Fourteen people are marked as marriage candidates** and all are on the deep
   dialogue tier. The number is still open in #137. Any player look can court
   any candidate (`12_marriage.md`).
7. **Deep tier: 19 of 30.** The 14 candidates, plus the hermit, the Wanderer,
   the courier, the carpenter and the hall steward. Courtship and branching
   conversations are released in layers (`18_dialogue.md`), so the load can be
   spread.
